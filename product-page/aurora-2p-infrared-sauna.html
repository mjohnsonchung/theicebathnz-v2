Ultra-Low EMF

0–3 mG

Milligauss — well below safety thresholds

Every Aurora sauna is built with ultra-low EMF heaters that measure between 0 and 3 milligauss — lower than most household appliances like hairdryers (40–100 mG) or laptops (5–20 mG). This isn't an optional upgrade. It's the standard on every unit we ship. Sit closer to the heaters, stay longer, and know the engineering was done right.

SAA Certified for New Zealand & Australia

The Aurora range carries SAA (Standards Australia Approval) certification, confirming compliance with electrical safety standards across Australia and New Zealand. Every unit is tested and certified before it leaves — giving you confidence that your sauna meets the same safety benchmarks required of all commercial electrical appliances sold in the region.
