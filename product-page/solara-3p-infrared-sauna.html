Ultra-Low EMF

Every unit tested.
Every shipment verified.

EMF — electromagnetic fields — are produced by all electrical devices. High-EMF exposure over long periods is a concern for many sauna users. Our carbon infrared panels are engineered to keep EMF between 0 and 3 milligauss, well below the threshold most guidelines consider safe.

Every shipment is independently tested before it leaves the factory. You receive a sauna that meets the same low-EMF standard every time — not just on a spec sheet, but in practice.

SAA Certified for New Zealand & Australia

The Solara 3P meets SAA electrical safety standards for use in New Zealand and Australia. Certified compliant, so you can be confident in both the safety and quality of the unit from day one.
