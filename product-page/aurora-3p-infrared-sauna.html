Safety Standard

Ultra-low EMF.
Standard, not optional.

Every Aurora sauna ships with EMF levels rated at 0–3 milligauss — lower than most household appliances. This isn't an upgrade or add-on. It's built into the heater design from the start.

EMF Rating

0–3 milligauss

For context, a typical hair dryer emits 60–200 mG, and a microwave oven 100–300 mG. The Aurora range sits at 0–3 mG — well below the threshold of everyday electronics. You can sit inside for extended sessions without concern.

Near Infrared (NIR)

700–1,400 nm

Penetrates the skin's surface layers. Supports cellular energy production and skin health. The closest wavelength to natural sunlight.

Mid Infrared (MIR)

1,400–3,000 nm

Reaches deeper into soft tissue. Promotes circulation and supports joint and muscle recovery.

Far Infrared (FIR)

3,000–10,000 nm

The deepest-penetrating wavelength. Raises core body temperature for a deep, detoxifying sweat at lower ambient temperatures.

Common Questions

Frequently
asked.

Regular sauna use has been linked to improved cardiovascular health, reduced muscle soreness, better sleep quality, and stress relief. Infrared saunas operate at lower ambient temperatures than traditional saunas while still raising your core body temperature — producing a deep, detoxifying sweat that many users find more comfortable and sustainable for longer sessions.

Traditional saunas heat the air around you (typically 70–100°C), which then heats your body. Infrared saunas use infrared light to heat your body directly at lower ambient temperatures (30–70°C). This means you can sweat deeply at a more comfortable temperature, for longer sessions, and with lower energy consumption. Full-spectrum infrared (near, mid, far) provides the broadest range of therapeutic wavelengths.

The Aurora infrared saunas reach up to 70°C. Most users find their preferred temperature between 45–60°C. The sauna reaches 40°C in around 10 minutes, and full operating temperature within 20–30 minutes. Because infrared heats your body directly rather than just the air, you'll start sweating well before the cabin reaches its maximum temperature.
