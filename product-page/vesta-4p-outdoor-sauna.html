Low EMF upgrade

What changes with the upgrade?

The Low EMF upgrade replaces the standard heater panels with far-infrared-only panels that produce between 0 and 3 milligauss — measured within an inch of the heater surface. The wiring is also shielded to minimise electromagnetic leakage.

  • Heater panels are swapped — standard full-spectrum panels replaced with far-infrared low EMF panels
  • Wiring is shielded for minimal electromagnetic leakage
  • Same cabin, same controls, same warranty — only the heaters change
  • Heat character shifts from intense and invigorating to gentle and soothing
  • Ideal for those who want to minimise EMF exposure without compromising on the sauna experience

Both heater types are safe and well within international guidelines. The choice comes down to personal preference — whether you want the deeper, more intense heat of full-spectrum, or the calmer, lower-EMF experience of far infrared.

SAA Certified for New Zealand & Australia

The Vesta range carries SAA (Standards Australia Approval) certification, confirming compliance with electrical safety standards across Australia and New Zealand. Every unit is tested and certified before it leaves — giving you confidence that your sauna meets the same safety benchmarks required of all commercial electrical appliances sold in the region.
