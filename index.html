Why Cold Therapy

Why New Zealand chooses cold.

Cold water immersion triggers a surge in norepinephrine — up to 300% above baseline — delivering sharper focus, elevated mood, and faster recovery that lasts all day. Elite athletes use ice baths to cut recovery time in half. Everyday users report better sleep, reduced inflammation, and a notable shift in mental resilience within two weeks.

Our ice bath tubs are engineered for New Zealand conditions: G90 galvanised steel that handles frost, rain, and UV without corroding. Paired with a precision chiller that holds your target temperature down to 3°C — no ice runs, no guesswork. Whether you're after a cold plunge tub for home use or a stainless steel ice bath for your gym, we deliver nationwide with a 1–2 year warranty.
