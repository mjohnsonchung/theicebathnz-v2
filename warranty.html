Warranty Policy

Effective: 26 July 2026

Looking After Your Product

Keeping your product in good condition is part of keeping your warranty valid. As the owner, you're expected to routinely check the internal parts of your unit — this includes the pump, barrel unions and UV system — and to keep on top of water chemistry as described in the owner's manual supplied with your product. Skipping this upkeep can cause damage that isn't covered, and neglecting these steps may void your warranty entirely.

Only chemicals sold or approved by The Ice Bath NZ (or by our authorised suppliers) should be used with your product. Using unapproved or third-party chemicals may also void your warranty.

Limitation of Liability

So far as New Zealand law allows, The Ice Bath NZ and its affiliates accept no responsibility for indirect, special, incidental or consequential losses of any kind. This covers, among other things, loss of business, lost income, loss of data or personal injury, however that loss might come about.

Where a product is bought for business or commercial use, the guarantees set out in the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 are treated as not applying.

Confirming that a product meets local council rules — building consents, resource consents, fencing requirements and the like — is the buyer's responsibility, not ours. Anything we tell you on these matters is general guidance only and doesn't account for your specific site or the rules in your region. The Ice Bath NZ won't cover any costs that flow from non-compliance or from a warranty claim, including changes to your installation, alterations to the site, removal or re-delivery charges, or labour.

Warranty Limits & How Claims Work

  • Original purchaser only. Every The Ice Bath NZ warranty is tied to the person who first bought the product and can't be passed on to anyone else.
  • No modifications. Changing or altering the product from how it was originally supplied will void the warranty.
  • This is the full warranty. The terms in this document are the only warranty The Ice Bath NZ provides. No spoken or implied warranties apply on top of it.
  • If a replacement is needed. In the uncommon event that a product has to be replaced, we'll supply the same model where it's still available (specifications may have been updated in the meantime). If that model has been discontinued, we'll issue store credit equal to what you originally paid.

Ice Bath Warranty

Tubs

Structure: 1-year structural warranty. The structural warranty applies solely to leaks in the main body of the tub. Surface marks, cosmetic flaws and general wear aren't included.

Timber parts: Not covered. Because timber is a natural material, how it holds up depends largely on use and care. Extended exposure to sun, rain or moisture — particularly if it hasn't been sealed — can cause colour changes, warping or mould. None of these are product faults, so they fall outside the warranty.

Caring for the timber: Keeping the timber in good shape is up to you, which means treating it regularly with wood wax oil or a similar product. Timber that isn't maintained may age or fail sooner than it otherwise would.

Tub Covers

Standard (non-timber) covers: Covered for 1 month from the delivery date.

What We Ask of You

  • Replace the water filter every one to three months (depending on how well water is maintained), using the replacement filters supplied with your unit and available through our website
  • Keep water chemistry correct at all times. Chlorine must never be used with our cooling systems
  • Use only genuine (OEM) parts and filters
  • Keep sand, debris and other contaminants out of the system

Conditions & Exclusions

This warranty holds only when the product is used and cared for the way The Ice Bath NZ sets out. It will not apply where any of the following occur:

  • Ground movement or other environmental shifts affecting the product
  • Maintenance of the tub, cooling system or sauna that is irregular or done incorrectly
  • Installation or care instructions not being followed
  • Installation on ground that is uneven, unstable or otherwise unsuitable
  • Poor water management or the use of the wrong chemicals
  • Grit, sand or foreign matter getting into the water, plumbing or pump
  • Running the equipment hotter than the manufacturer recommends
  • Repairs, alterations or modifications carried out without authorisation
  • Electrical work done by someone who isn't a qualified professional
  • Electrical faults such as blowouts, surges or voltage spikes
  • A dedicated electrical circuit for the unit not being maintained
  • Filters, strainers or pipework becoming blocked or dirty
  • Damage from pests, termites, severe weather or other events beyond reasonable control
  • Commercial use (unless we've specifically agreed to it)
  • Pipes freezing in cold seasonal conditions
  • Resale of the product — warranties stay with the original purchaser only
  • Use of non-genuine replacement parts or filters
  • Excess dirt, sand or debris being introduced into the system
  • Any repair not carried out or approved by The Ice Bath NZ or an authorised partner

Support & Repairs

If something goes wrong and it's covered here, The Ice Bath NZ will arrange repairs and/or replacement parts at no charge to you. Please note, though:

  • The cost of shipping a product back to us for inspection or servicing isn't covered
  • On-site servicing is available in Auckland only. Anywhere else, a warranty assessment or repair may mean returning the product to us or having it evaluated remotely

Making a Claim

If a fault appears, let us know in writing within 7 days of noticing it. Your claim should include:

  • A clear description of the fault
  • Photos or video showing the problem
  • Proof of purchase
